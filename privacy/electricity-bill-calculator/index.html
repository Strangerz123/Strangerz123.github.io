This Privacy Policy explains how the Electricity Bill Calculator app handles information when you use the app.

The app does not require you to create an account simply to use its bill-calculation features. If a particular version of the app includes a feature that asks for personal information, that information should only be used for the purpose described in that feature.

The app may rely on third-party platform or service providers for functions such as app distribution, diagnostics, analytics, or advertising, depending on the version installed. Those providers may process technical information such as device identifiers, app activity, approximate location derived from network information, crash data, or advertising identifiers according to their own privacy policies.

Permissions

The app only requests device permissions that are needed for features available in the installed version. You can review or change app permissions through your Android device settings.

Data retention and security

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided by a child, please contact the developer through the contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer using the developer contact details listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
