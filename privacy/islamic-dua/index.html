Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Islamic Dua app handles information when you use the app.

App content and local features

Features such as browsing duas, searching, saving favorites, reading offline content, and using app display settings are intended to work without requiring an account.

Prayer reminders and related settings

If you enable reminders or other notification features, the app may store the related settings on your device so the requested reminders can operate. You can disable notifications through the app or Android settings.

Advertising and third-party services

Some versions of the app may use third-party services, including advertising or Google platform services. These providers may process technical information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, crash data, diagnostic information, or approximate location derived from network information according to their own privacy policies.

Personal information

The app does not require account registration for its core dua and reminder features. We do not ask you to submit sensitive personal information as a condition of using those core features.

Permissions

The app may request Android permissions needed for enabled features, such as notifications. You can review or change permissions at any time through your device settings.

Data retention and security

Where settings or favorites are stored locally, they remain on your device unless removed by you, cleared through app settings, or deleted when the app is uninstalled. We use reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, but no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided by a child, please contact the developer using the contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the developer contact details listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
